| Index | Name | Seed Default | Meaning |
|-------|------|-------------|---------|

**The current working bench gains** (applied via .parm overrides, identical in both `indi_pi.parm` and `indi_wls.parm`) are:

| Index | Name | Value | Meaning |
|-------|------|-------|---------|
| 24 | THX_INDI_KP | 1.5 | Position gain (omega_n=1.2 rad/s) |
| 25 | THX_INDI_KV | 2.2 | Velocity gain (zeta=0.9) |
| 26 | THX_INDI_KW | 8.0 | Angular rate gain (zeta=1.0 with KR=16) |
| 27 | THX_INDI_KR | 16.0 | Attitude error gain (omega_n=4 rad/s, NEGATED) |

**ALL gain values are REFERENCE_SEED_NOT_MEASURED.** Both the firmware defaults and the bench-tuned overrides are seed values. None are based on system identification or physical measurements.

**HARD RULE 2**: PI and WLS parm files differ ONLY in THX_ALLOC_MODE (0 vs 1). All INDI gains, filter cutoffs, THX_ACT_MODEL=1, THX_QP_MAX_ITER=40, and alloc weights are identical.

**Verification**:
```bash
diff <(grep THX_INDI config/indi_pi.parm) <(grep THX_INDI config/indi_wls.parm)
# No difference: Kp=1.5, Kv=2.2, Kw=8.0, KR=16.0 identical
```

The KR gain is applied with a **negative sign** in the rotational INDI law (Round 4a fix):
`nu_omega = Kw*(omega_r - omega) - KR*e_R`

**INDI law**:
- Translational: `nu_v = a_r + Kv*(v_r - v) + Kp*(p_r - p)`, then `f_d = R^T*(nu_v - g*e3)`, `Delta F = m*(f_d - f_f)`, `F_d = F_f + Delta F`
- Rotational: `nu_omega = Kw*(omega_r - omega) - KR*e_R`
- PI mode: Fx demand zeroed (pitch-based forward flight)
- WLS mode: Fx computed incrementally with rate limiting (tilt-based forward flight)
- KI = 0.0f (attitude integral disabled; INDI has integral-like action through incremental structure)

## 4. Fx Strategy

**PI baseline (alloc_mode=0)**: The controller zeros Fx in the wrench demand. Forward thrust comes from the gravity component of the tilted thrust vector: F_x = m*g*sin(theta). Forward speed depends indirectly on the pitch angle from the trajectory reference.

**WLS proposed (alloc_mode=1)**: Fx is computed incrementally (`Delta_Fx`) and rate-limited. The QP allocator tilts rotors to produce net body-frame Fx (HARD RULE 3: forward flight from tilting rotors, not prescribed beta(V)). fx_frac=0.50 for WLS (uses more of the Fx budget).

**Limitations of Fx=0 (PI baseline)**:
- Altitude loss during acceleration (~60m -> ~7m): vertical thrust component reduced by pitch angle
- Speed overshoot (22.8 m/s at 20 m/s target): forward speed not directly controlled
- Below ground at 25 m/s cruise (-1.8m during acceleration)
- Inability to complete coordinated turns: no lateral force, altitude collapses during banked turn
